- Centering + "Should I grade it?" (Oct 6): `src/app/centering.tsx` (full-screen modal). Opened from the card page button or Should I grade it? in the long-press sheet. It takes a 1600 px photo and runs `PullVision.measureCentering` in a light WebView page (`MEASURE_PAGE_HTML`, no tesseract and no index). Results are L/R and T/B splits on the flattened card plus a PSA cap from `utils/centering.ts`: 10 is safe at 55/45 and gray up to 60/40, 9 is 60–65, 8 is 65–70 and 7 is 70–75. `GradeCalculator` compares raw against PSA 10/9/8 minus `settings.gradingCost`, default $30 per card. It needs a contrasting surface, and full-art cards with no border can't be measured. How it works: `SCANNER_ARCHITECTURE.md` → Centering.
- Foils (Oct 6): `src/three/cardFinish.ts` maps rarity to a finish: plain, window, full, textured, gold, rainbow, illustration, special (SIR), chrome (Futuristic Rare), shiny, pikachu (30th Celebration), reverse or cosmos. The set's era picks the holo pattern: starlight, cosmos, tinsel, sheen, waterweb, line or mirage. `foilMaterial.ts` holds a `LOOKS` table per finish (area, pattern, etch, glitter, tint, metal). Test rig: headless Edge renders the real material on card images at several tilts (scratch `foilrig/`, `node rig.mjs spec.json out.png`). The 30th Pikachu has a swirl on the background, dots on the art and a dotted silver border. Everything in 30th Celebration is foil.
- Default theme = Frutiger Aero (`aero`, shown as "Default"): `AeroSky` background, `Gloss` highlight on accent buttons, `theme.gloss`. `DESIGN_VERSION` 3 switched existing installs once. Graphite is still in Appearance, and built-in theme choices now persist.
- Card swiping: list screens call `setBrowseList` (`services/cardBrowse.ts`) before opening a card. The card page wraps `CardPage` (keyed by id) and swaps cards with `router.setParams({ id, entry, from })`. `CardSwipe` handles the gesture and the "3 of N" pager. `card/[id]` has `fullScreenGestureEnabled: false` so a right swipe doesn't pop.
- Free placement (Oct 7): `components/FreeBoard.tsx` runs the Collection page (`boards.collection`) and the card page (`boards.card`, one layout shared by every card). Hold any widget for 500 ms (140 ms while editing) and it lifts. Drag it anywhere, and pinch with two fingers to resize it (40% to full width, snapping to ½, ⅔ and full). Positions are saved as fractions of the width plus a y used for ordering. Widgets settle upward (react-grid-layout style) and never overlap, and a dashed outline shows where the lifted one will land. The others are frozen at their positions while one is lifted. The red minus hides a widget, and the edit bar (`BoardEditBar`) has Tidy up (everything full width), Reset and chips to bring hidden widgets back. `useBoard(id)` wires a board to settings. Raw touch events plus `onMoveShouldSetResponderCapture` take the touch away from child pressables once a widget lifts. There's no auto-scroll near the screen edges yet.
- Boards v2 (Oct 7): pinching follows the axis. Sideways sets width (½, ⅔ and full snaps), up and down sets `hs`, and diagonal sets both. Widgets with `stretch` (both market charts and the collection chart) get `heightScale` and grow taller. Other widgets crop to 30–100% with a fade. Dragging within 90 px of the top or bottom edges auto-scrolls through `useAutoScroll` (an `AutoScroller` object kept in state, because the lint rules don't allow refs to be read during render). `useBoard` keeps a 30-step undo history, shown as Undo in the edit bar. Whole pages are boards: card (`DetailBoard id="card"`, including the picture and title), sealed (`id="sealed"`), the set header (`set`), the Search home (`discover`) and Collection (with the toolbar as a widget that can't be hidden). New widgets slot in before the next saved one.
- Binder (Oct 7): `/binders` lists them and `/binder/[id]` opens one. A binder is a cover plus 9-pocket pages, with one blank page always at the end. Pages are a stack turned by a PanResponder (`flip` value, rotateY around the spine, cover on top). A ScrollView pager broke stacking on web. Hold a card in the tray or in a pocket to lift it (`Holdable`), drop it on a pocket to place or swap it, or on the tray to take it out. Holding at a screen edge turns the page. "Fill empty pockets" adds the rest in set and number order. Data lives in `BinderProvider` (`pullcheck.binders.v1`), with slot index → collection item key. Art in `assets/binder/` is rendered in Blender from `tools/blender/binder.py` (bpy 5.2, Cycles CPU). Run `python -I tools/blender/binder.py <outdir> hero|front|glb`, then convert to WebP. `assets/binder/binder.glb` is the 3D model for later use.
- New tags (Oct 7): `seenAt` on collection items. `showsNewTag` = (fresh or added this week) and not seen since `lastAddedAt`. Opening an item (from Collection, or any card page) queues it in `services/seen.ts`. The Collection tab marks it seen 450 ms after it regains focus, so `NewBadge` plays its take-off animation where you can see it.
- Undo (Oct 7): removing an item (or setting its quantity to 0) keeps it in `removed`. `UndoToast` shows for 6 s under the top bar, and Undo puts it back in the same spot (`restore` action).
- Tabs (Oct 6): Collection · Scan · Search, and the app opens on Collection. Collection is now `(tabs)/index.tsx` and Scan is `(tabs)/scan.tsx`. `/` means Collection, and the Pull "end without saving" button goes to `/scan`.
- Sets wiggle (Oct 6): hold a set on the Sets screen for 450 ms and every set wiggles. Drag the held set to a new spot and it saves when you let go. While wiggling, a 180 ms hold lifts another set, tapping does nothing, and Done exits.
